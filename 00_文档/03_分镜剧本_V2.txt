硬性纪律（每一镜都已按此设计）
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. 10秒红线：所有镜头成片时长≤9秒。每镜按10秒生成，成片截取中段
   稳定部分（掐头去尾各0.5-1秒），分镜标注时长=成片时长。
2. 一镜一动作：每镜只写一个主动作+至多两个次动作；复杂交互拆镜。
3. 双对比切换律：相邻两镜必须同时改变[景别]与[运镜性质]
   （固定/跟移/手持/环绕/推拉 属不同性质）。
4. 同镜人物≤3：群像镜头（S21后厨/S26广场）用中远景+剪影+手部
   特写插入解决，不给AI超过3张需要追踪的脸。
5. 文字三不：街牌、邀请函、落款文字一律后期贴字，不交给AI生成。
6. 台词上限：单镜台词≤15字（10秒内两句话的极限）。
7. 年龄盲区：视频提示词中不写"8岁男孩"，用"穿赭黄色短袖的小身
   影"按服装+动作+角色描述（防止漂移与内容过滤）。
8. 表演总则（来自人物小传）：阿澄前期先看作品再看人，后期先看
   人的动作再动笔；思考=拇指摩挲画本边缘；珍姐递盘前先找眼神；
   成叔=停/看/教/等/点头五拍；阿敏受挫抿嘴调呼吸再站回原位。

----------------------------------------------------------------
每镜提示词按以下十维组织，EN为粘贴主版本（末尾追加统一前缀块）：
① REF声明：垫图分角色——人物"100% matches the reference"（身份锁）、
  场景"environment only"（模型可延展）
② 镜头序言：cut Ns · gen 10s · 16:9 · ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT · I2V
③ INTENT：一句话戏剧弧（这镜为什么存在）
④ FRAME：FOV档位+构图坐标（主体x=%、面向、负空间留给什么）
⑤ CAMERA：命名运镜+精确速率（每N秒漂移约M%画面宽）+镜头负锁
  （NO dolly/tilt/orbit/zoom…）+handheld micro-shake是否叠加
⑥ IDENTITY/STATE：角色锁+跨镜携带状态（围裙/画本/水珠）
⑦ AMBIENT & COLOR：光位方向+全片色彩策略（唯一暖饱和=活人与生活
  物件：阿乐赭黄/蒸汽微金；其余钴蓝与暖白域）
⑧ PHASE 1..N（成片时钟，可含变速0.5x/1x）：每拍=面部解剖级微动作
  或材质物理（源自人物小传物理化）；拍点=成片时长
⑨ MOTION HIERARCHY四层分离+LOCK负锁+FINAL FRAME+SFX逐拍
⑩ CN对照（即梦精简版）+风险预案

S01｜0:00-0:05｜5秒｜大远景(ELS)｜缓慢推近 Slow Dolly In
----------------------------------------------------------------
画面：黑场渐亮。清晨的澳门老城全貌——骑楼、教堂尖顶、塔影在薄雾
中层层铺开，整体笼罩蓝白青灰色调（全片色彩基调此镜定调）。几只
白鸽掠过前景屋脊。画面右下角一堵老墙局部露出蓝白瓷砖，晨光在其
釉面上流动（核心符号的第一次出现，不点破）。
声音：远处教堂钟一记；城市苏醒的底噪（卷帘门、电车铃、市声）由
零起混；主题曲A段进入（法朵吉他×古筝，慢6/8拍）。
转场：硬切。
资产：S-02（远景版）。
AI要点（V3 全息提示词）：
【垫图】S07_城市大远景_晨雾态_v1.png（environment only，模型可延展雾层）
【EN·全息版】
SHOT S01 · cut 5s · gen 10s · 16:9 · ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT · I2V single ref
INTENT: a city waking before its people do — stillness breathing toward first light.
FRAME: 107° FOV; horizon on upper third; three mist layers (rooftop band / church-spike band / tower silhouette band); azulejo wall lower-right as the only foreground texture; upper-left negative space reserved for the arriving glow.
CAMERA: slow dolly-in toward town center, ~4% frame width over the full 5s, constant rate, subtle handheld breathing micro-shake. NO tilt, NO orbit, NO zoom, NO lateral drift.
IDENTITY/STATE: no characters; the town keeps its layout, tile colors and skyline silhouette throughout — no building added or removed.
AMBIENT & COLOR: pre-sunrise cool cobalt field; the ONLY warm accent is the one-degree horizon glow in phase 3; mist desaturates mid-depth.
PHASE 1 (0-2s, 1x): mist banks drift slowly between rooftop layers, left-to-right, ~5% frame width per second; nothing else moves.
PHASE 2 (2-4s, 1x): a loose flock of 8-12 pigeons crosses mid-frame left-to-right at a relaxed wingbeat; mist continues beneath them.
PHASE 3 (4-5s, 1x): horizon-left glow warms exactly one degree; on the low wall lower-right, azulejo glaze catches its first specular highlight and holds.
MOTION HIERARCHY — subject: none · internal: none · camera: dolly-in + micro-shake · environment: mist drift, pigeon flock, one glaze highlight.
LOCK: no vehicles, no figures, no window lights; mist never fully clears; glow warms ONE degree only — no sunrise burst.
FINAL FRAME: town center slightly nearer, mist half-lifted, one warm edge at left, glaze lit lower-right.
SFX: one distant church bell ~1.5s; faint waking-city bed; wing flutter mid phase 2.
【CN·对照】意图：城比人先醒。107°远景，雾三层，缓推约4%画面宽/5秒+手持微颤，禁倾斜环绕变焦。拍点：0-2雾漂→2-4鸽群8-12只横穿→4-5左缘暖一度+右下釉面高光。锁：无人无车无灯亮、雾不清、光只暖一度。声：1.5s远钟、鸽翅。
【风险预案】雾糊→降雾密度重出/无雾版后期叠；鸽群散乱→减到6只重roll

（灰蓝衬衫、米白内搭）立在长工作台前，手中展开一封信。他先低头

声音：纸张展开声、空间混响里的脚步、窗外鸽哨。音乐延续。
转场：硬切。
资产：C-01 / S-01（昼态）/ P-07邀请函。
AI要点（V3 全息提示词）：
【垫图】S01_艺术空间_昼态_v1.png（environment only）+ C01_a_立姿持信_v1.png（100% matches the reference）+ P07_邀请函_留白版_v1.png
【EN·全息版】
SHOT S02 · cut 5s · gen 10s · 16:9 · ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT · I2V, character ref + location ref
INTENT: a man who still relates to paper before people — the letter finishes him, not the room.
FRAME: 63° FOV; designer stands on right third, x=62-70%; tall window upper-left pours a light shaft crossing mid-frame; letter held low, head tilted down toward its last line; dust motes occupy the shaft between camera and subject.
CAMERA: locked static, zero movement, NO handheld shake — stillness reads as reverence. NO push, NO drift.
IDENTITY/STATE: C01 locked (grey-blue shirt, off-white inner, side-parted black hair); state carried: letter already open, reading the final line; no watch, no bag.
AMBIENT & COLOR: morning key from window upper-left, cool white; letter's cream is the only warm note in frame; background bookshelves fall into soft shadow.
PHASE 1 (0-2s, 1x): his eyes finish the last line; one slow deliberate blink — eyelids close and open over ~0.8s.
PHASE 2 (2-4s, 1x): chin lifts one degree; gaze travels from letter to window light; shoulders stay exactly where they were.
PHASE 3 (4-5s, 1x): dust motes drift downward-through the shaft at varied speeds; he is now looking at light, not paper.
MOTION HIERARCHY — subject: blink, chin lift · internal: eye-line travel letter→window · camera: none · environment: dust motes in shaft.
LOCK: he does NOT fold the letter, does NOT step, does NOT smile; shirt/hair/face identical to C01_a throughout; letter text stays unreadable cream.
FINAL FRAME: profile toward window, letter resting low, shaft of dust between lens and man.
SFX: paper settling at 0.5s; distant pigeon whistle ~3s; room tone.

SHOT S03 · cut 3s · gen 10s · 16:9 · ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT · I2V single prop ref
INTENT: the invitation as an object of ceremony — paper has weight.
FRAME: top-down macro, card fills 80% frame slightly rotated 3°; wax seal lower-right quadrant; blank text area occupies center-left; soft shadow edge bottom.
CAMERA: slow push-in toward the blank text area, ~5% width over 3s, constant; NO rotation, NO drift, NO parallax swing.
IDENTITY/STATE: card cream texture, emboss depth and seal color locked to P07; text area stays BLANK ceramic-clean.
AMBIENT & COLOR: soft key upper-left; seal's cobalt is the only saturated point; card reads warm-white against cool shadow edge.
PHASE 1 (0-1s, 1x): paper edge trembles twice, amplitude ~1mm, like a breath passed under a door.
PHASE 2 (1-2s, 1x): light sweeps slowly across the embossing, shadow depth in the pressed lines deepening then easing.
PHASE 3 (2-3s, 1x): a soft specular highlight lands on the wax seal and holds; edge tremble has stopped — the card settles.
MOTION HIERARCHY — subject: none · internal: none · camera: slow push · environment: edge tremble, light sweep, one highlight.
LOCK: NO text appears anywhere; seal does not melt or shine bloom; card never rotates beyond the initial 3°.
FINAL FRAME: text area marginally closer, seal holding its highlight, everything still.
SFX: two soft paper ticks; near-silence under.
【CN·对照】意图：请柬即仪式。俯拍微距满幅，缓推向留白区5%宽/3秒。拍点：0-1纸缘颤两次→1-2光扫压印→2-3火漆高光落定。锁：任何文字都不出现。声：两声纸轻响。
【风险预案】AI自加字→局部重绘擦除（文字铁律）；颤动过猛→度幅锁1mm
