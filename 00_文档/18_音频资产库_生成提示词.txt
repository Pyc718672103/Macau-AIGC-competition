二、音乐资产 M（Suno 生成，提示词直接可用）
================================================================
工具：Suno（Instrumental 模式，关掉人声）。风格栏+描述栏分开填。

M01 主题曲·A段（用于 0:00-1:00，S01起进）
  风格栏：Portuguese fado guitar, Chinese guzheng, chamber folk,
          instrumental, slow 6/8
  描述栏：A gentle duet of Portuguese fado guitar and Chinese
  guzheng, slow 6/8 lilt around 66 bpm, A minor, wistful but warm.
  The two instruments trade a simple melodic phrase like a
  conversation, sparse and intimate, plenty of silence between
  notes. No percussion, no vocals. Ends open, unresolved.
  时长目标：60秒；结构：主题句呈示→对答→轻轻收在延音上。

M02 主题曲·B段变奏（用于 2:03-3:05，S28起进）
  风格栏：同M01，加 soft strings
  描述栏：The same fado-guitar-and-guzheng theme returns with a
  soft warm string pad underneath, slightly fuller but still
  restrained, slow 6/8. It grows gently bar by bar, never loud,
  like a street gradually waking. No drums, no vocals.
  时长目标：60秒。生成时把M01作为参考/继续（同曲变奏）。

M03 主题曲·终句（用于 3:44-3:50，S46-S47）
  描述栏：Final cadence of this theme, resolving warmly at last,
  then a single sustained guzheng harmonic with a long natural
  decay, extremely sparse, nothing else.
  时长目标：10秒。可与M01/M02同批生成剪辑取尾。

M04 醒狮鼓·远景动机轨（用于 S05/S09/S13 的远处鼓点种子）
  描述栏：A traditional Chinese lion dance drum heard from far
  away, muffled and distant, slow steady single beats like a
  heartbeat through streets, lo-fi distance, loopable, no other
  instruments.
  时长目标：30秒循环。

M05 醒狮鼓·完整段（用于 S26 情绪顶点，3:50-3:57）
  描述栏：Full Cantonese lion dance drum pattern with light
  cymbals, energetic and celebratory but neighborhood-scale, not
  stadium. Clear steady pulse a community would clap to.
  时长目标：15秒。
  备注：AI生成鼓段节奏可能不稳——优先用CC0实录采样（见SFX13）。

M06 展厅夜氛围底（用于第五幕垫底，可选）
  描述栏：Very soft night ambience, faint distant city hum,
  gentle room reverb, occasional far footsteps on wood floor.
  时长目标：60秒。也可用SFX12实录替代。
